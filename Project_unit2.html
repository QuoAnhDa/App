SUMMARY

What We Found

🎵 Differences

Young people like modern music, online videos and big cities. Older people prefer folk music, TV news and quiet villages.

👗 Clothes & hobbies

Teenagers wear casual, trendy clothes. Grandparents choose traditional clothes and enjoy gardening, not games.

❤️ Similarities

All three generations love family meals, Lunar New Year and spending time together. Almost everyone said a gap exists, but it is normal.

📊 Result: about 10 out of 11 group members said there is a generation gap in their family.

SOLUTIONS

How to Deal with the Generation Gap

Children should listen to their parents and grandparents.

Parents should try to understand young people’s interests.

We must respect older people and follow good family traditions.

Teenagers don’t have to like the same music, but they mustn’t be rude.

Families have to spend time talking and eating together.

Grandparents shouldn’t judge, and the young shouldn’t ignore them.
